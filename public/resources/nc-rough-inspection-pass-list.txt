NC Rough-Inspection Pass List
Southern Cities Construction

This free field sheet is meant to reduce avoidable rough-inspection failures on residential jobs in North Carolina.

Before the inspection request
- Confirm the permit card / permit number is available.
- Confirm the scope is ready for the exact inspection being requested.
- Confirm no unfinished work is hiding behind walls or ceilings that should still be open.
- Confirm the responsible contact can answer inspector questions.

Framing rough checks
- Confirm framing changes match approved plans.
- Confirm headers, beams, and posts are installed as designed.
- Confirm fastening, straps, anchors, and hold-downs are in place where required.
- Confirm stair and landing framing dimensions are correct if applicable.
- Confirm draft-stopping and fire-blocking conditions are being addressed.

Electrical rough checks
- Confirm box count and placement match intended fixtures and devices.
- Confirm cable protection plates are installed where required.
- Confirm homeruns, circuits, and panel labeling logic are organized.
- Confirm required AFCI / GFCI planning has been accounted for.
- Confirm bonding / grounding path is being handled correctly.

Plumbing rough checks
- Confirm venting path is complete.
- Confirm drains are supported and sloped properly.
- Confirm cleanouts are accessible where required.
- Confirm supply lines are protected where they pass through framing.
- Confirm tubs, shower valves, and fixture rough-ins are at correct height and location.

Mechanical rough checks
- Confirm duct layout matches the intended equipment and room count.
- Confirm returns are sized and placed correctly.
- Confirm required clearances are maintained.
- Confirm bath fan and dryer exhaust paths are correct.
- Confirm refrigerant, condensate, and equipment support details are handled.

General readiness checks
- Confirm work areas are accessible and safe.
- Confirm no debris is blocking inspection visibility.
- Confirm corrective punch items from prior inspections are fully resolved.
- Confirm photos are taken before concealment.

If something is questionable
- Stop and fix it before calling inspection.
- Do not assume the inspector will ignore a visible miss.
- Recheck the plan set and trade scope.
