## 3. Questions

### Custom questions (qualifiers — keep to these 2)
**Q1 — Multiple choice:**
> Which best describes you?
- Wholesaler
- Fix & flip investor
- Real estate agent
- Buy & hold investor
- New / still learning

**Q2 — Multiple choice:**
> How many deals have you closed in the last 12 months?
- 0
- 1–3
- 4–9
- 10+

### Prefill contact info (Meta auto-fills from the user's profile)
- ✅ Full name
- ✅ Email
- ✅ Phone number  *(biggest friction lever — keep it for better follow-up; remove it first if your cost-per-lead is too high)*

> ⚠️ Don't add more than these. Every extra field raises your cost-per-lead. Qualify deeper in the email follow-up, not the form.

## 6. Wire up the follow-up (do this in GHL / your CRM)
The completion-screen button gives instant access, but **also** trigger an email so they keep it:
1. Connect the Lead Form to your CRM (GHL → Meta Lead Ads integration, or Zapier).
2. On new lead → send the PDF + start the nurture sequence.
3. Tag by Q1 answer (Wholesaler / Investor / Agent) so the follow-up matches who they are.

> Direct download + emailed copy = no "I never got it" leaks, and you capture the lead in your CRM for retargeting/nurture.
