# Southern Cities Construction — Printable SOP Cards

Each card fits one page. Use the same skeleton:
- Service
- Promise
- Model
- SC owns
- Vendor lane
- Inputs
- Deliverable
- QA
- TAT
- Risk
- Never outsource
- Client update language

Operating rule on every card: Southern Cities owns judgment. Vendors do production only. Final delivery and accountability stay with Southern Cities.

---

## Card 1 — Home Assessment
- Promise: Clear next-step assessment before the wrong spend happens
- Model: B
- SC owns: site judgment, condition risk, recommendation
- Vendor lane: field photo runner, remote admin
- Inputs: address, photos, issue summary, goals
- Deliverable: SC-written assessment with priority list and next step
- QA: visible issues addressed, risks flagged, no false certainty, next step clear
- TAT: 2 business days
- Risk: Medium
- Never outsource: assessment judgment
- Client update: We are reviewing your property details and preparing your assessment summary now.

## Universal QA gate (every card)
- Scope match
- Assumptions explicit
- Risks called out
- Pricing supportable
- Recommendations attributed only to SC
- Next step clear
- No vendor branding
- No implied promises
- Escalations resolved
- Archived properly

## Universal client communication rules
- Always say: Southern Cities is reviewing/coordinating
- Never say: outsourced team or third-party vendor
- Final recommendations: only from Southern Cities
- Final accountability: Southern Cities
